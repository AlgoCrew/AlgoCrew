Boundaries

What we do, and what we do not.

  • Map requirements to cloud configuration.
  • Implement and test technical controls.
  • Organize evidence for your assessor.
  • Work alongside your auditor and counsel.
  • Certify, attest or audit.
  • Give legal advice.
  • Guarantee an audit outcome.
  • Write your organizational policies for you.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can you make us compliant?

No one can promise that. We implement and evidence the technical controls. Your assessor decides compliance.

We answer to more than one framework. Do we do this twice?

No. Many controls overlap. We map each control once and show every requirement it supports.

Do you work with our auditor?

Yes. We prepare evidence in the form they ask for and answer technical questions about the environment.
