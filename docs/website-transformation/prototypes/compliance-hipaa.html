Who it applies to

Organizations that handle health information.

HIPAA applies to covered entities such as providers, health plans and clearinghouses, and to the business associates that handle protected health information for them.

If you are a covered entity, or a business associate that creates, receives, maintains or transmits electronic protected health information for one, the Security Rule applies to the systems that hold it. Your counsel decides whether that is you. This summary is not legal advice.

Shared responsibility

The provider secures the cloud. You secure what you run in it.

Control map

Security Rule technical safeguards, as cloud configuration.

Each safeguard maps to cloud settings and to the evidence those settings produce.

§164.312(a)

Access control

Unique identities, least-privilege roles, time-bound privileged access, encryption at rest with managed keys.

Evidence: role assignments, access reviews

§164.312(b)

Audit controls

Activity and data-access logs collected centrally, protected from change and retained to your policy.

Evidence: log configuration, retention settings

§164.312(c)

Integrity

Versioning, immutable backups, change control through pipelines and tested restores.

Evidence: restore test records, change history

§164.312(d)

Authentication

Single sign-on, multi-factor authentication and workload identities in place of shared credentials.

Evidence: authentication policies

§164.312(e)

Transmission security

TLS enforced in transit, private connectivity between services and segmented networks.

Evidence: policy compliance state

Continuous

Policy as code

Guardrails that block non-conforming resources and report drift.

Boundaries

What we do not do.

  • We do not certify you. There is no official HIPAA certification.
  • We do not give legal advice or decide whether you are a covered entity or business associate.
  • We do not write your administrative policies or run workforce training.
  • We do not guarantee the outcome of an audit or investigation.

FAQ

HIPAA questions.

Is Azure, AWS or Google Cloud "HIPAA compliant"?

Providers offer services that are eligible for HIPAA workloads under their agreement. How you configure and use them is what your assessor looks at.

Will you sign a Business Associate Agreement with us?

Answer depends on business confirmation (V-22). Until then: "Ask us about contractual terms."

We also need PIPEDA or GDPR. Can the same controls serve both?

Many technical controls overlap. We map each control once and show which requirements it supports.
