When Azure fits

Where we recommend Azure.

  • Your identity already lives in Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft 365.
  • You run Windows Server, SQL Server or .NET workloads you want to modernize.
  • You need Canadian regions: Canada Central and Canada East.
  • Your security team works in Microsoft Defender and Microsoft Sentinel.

Frameworks on Azure

How Azure handles the frameworks you answer to.

HIPAA

Microsoft offers a Business Associate Agreement covering in-scope services. We build on those services and configure the safeguards.

 GDPR

EU regions and Microsoft's data protection terms. We implement access, encryption and logging measures.

 PCI DSS

Azure's attestation covers the platform. Your workloads stay in scope, so we segment and harden the cardholder-data environment.

 PIPEDA

Canadian regions are available when you choose in-country residency. We implement the safeguards.

Our roleWe implement and evidence technical controls. Certification, attestation and legal interpretation are the role of your auditor, assessor or counsel.
